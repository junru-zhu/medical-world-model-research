# Provisional Venue Plan

Verified: 2026-09-12  
Primary target: Journal of Biomedical Informatics  
Article type: Review article — Methodological Review

## Why This Venue

The review's contribution is methodological rather than disease-specific. It evaluates how a class of biomedical informatics methods should be defined, validated, and reported. The manuscript also aims to produce generalizable lessons, applicability boundaries, and an operational evaluation codebook.

## Current Author-Guide Requirements

- Structured abstract of no more than 350 words using Objective, Methods, Results, and Conclusion.
- Main manuscript body of no more than 6,000 words, excluding abstract, references, appendices, figures, and tables.
- No more than eight figures and tables in total.
- Maximum of six keywords.
- A Statement of Significance table of no more than 100 words using:
  - Problem or Issue;
  - What Is Already Known;
  - What This Paper Adds.
- A mandatory graphical abstract, submitted separately; the journal specifies
  a minimum size of 531 × 1,328 pixels or proportionally larger and expects it
  to remain readable at approximately 5 × 13 cm.
- Numbered references in order of appearance; consistent formatting is acceptable at initial submission.
- Methodological reviews should focus on a method or approach and critically examine its uses, successes, limitations, inappropriate-use conditions, and future directions.
- The final manuscript must disclose generative-AI-assisted writing under the
  exact heading `Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in
  the writing process`.
- A competing-interest statement is required even when there are no interests
  to declare.
- CRediT author-contribution roles are required.
- The guide expects substantial involvement of healthcare professionals in
  all submissions.

Official guide:

https://www.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-biomedical-informatics/1532-0464/guide-for-authors

## Venue Risks

- Imaging, ultrasound, and surgical examples must support a general informatics methodology rather than read as a device or image-processing survey.
- The manuscript needs documented healthcare-professional expertise in
  interpretation and authorship.
- The review must complete the evidence extraction and operational codebook to establish methodological novelty.
- Author-supplied funding, competing-interest, CRediT, affiliation,
  corresponding-author, acknowledgments, and suggested-reviewer information
  remain incomplete.

## Alternative

IEEE Reviews in Biomedical Engineering is a secondary option if the paper expands into a longer engineering review with three to five original educational figures, a comprehensive reference base, and an Outstanding Questions box.
