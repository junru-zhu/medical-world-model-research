## Additional submission fields

The following are not safely inferable and must be supplied by the authors:

- conflicts of interest;
- author contributions;
- healthcare-professional involvement and roles;
- acknowledgments;
- corresponding-author and affiliation details;
- funding and funder role;
- stable public supplement, protocol, code, and data-artifact locations.

## PRISMA-S priorities

The search log already records database/resource names, full queries, dates,
per-source yields, deduplication keys, retrieval caps, API failures, citation
chasing, and the arXiv fallback. Before submission:

1. rerun the authenticated engineering and citation databases;
2. have the complete strategy peer reviewed by an information specialist;
3. freeze and archive every final query and export;
4. document the final search-update date and any newly included records; and
5. provide the final deduplication and publication-lineage ledger.
