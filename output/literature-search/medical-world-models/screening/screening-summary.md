were database duplicates and four were unique.

The original operational phrase/domain filter routed 5,335 records away from
the initial screen. A 170-record audit detected eligible studies in those
records. The correction therefore screened the audit sample, 86 additional
alternate-term records from the original search, all 5,079 residual filter
exclusions, and 944 records from fresh alternate-term retrieval.

## Screening and Lineage

Each route used two separate AI-assisted title/abstract assessments, dual
full-text review for conservatively advanced records, source-grounded
adjudication, inclusion quality control, and conceptual-equivalence review.

Cross-source publication-lineage review compared titles, authors, identifiers,
dates, preprint and journal relationships, corrections, withdrawals, and
retractions. It retained 93 distinct publications: 85 empirical studies and
eight reviews. The 40 publications absent from the former development snapshot
comprised 35 empirical studies and five reviews.

## Extraction and Coding

All 35 novel empirical studies completed two extraction and MedWM-Eval passes
plus source-grounded adjudication. The corrected canonical corpus contains:

- 85 aligned extraction records;
- 85 adjudicated MedWM-Eval records;
- 85 records in each separate coding pass;
- regenerated reliability, sensitivity, methodological-appraisal, and
  claim-provenance analyses; and
- a 93-publication reference ledger.

The canonical package is under `../final-corpus/`. The current manuscript and
figures use the corrected counts.

## Remaining Submission Gates

The review still requires:

1. qualified human dual verification of screening, extraction, and coding;
2. an authenticated IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Scopus or Web of
   Science update;
3. information-specialist review of the search strategy;
4. clinical, causal-inference, control, and measurement review of MedWM-Eval;
5. healthcare-professional involvement in clinical interpretation; and
6. author metadata, competing interests, funding, and CRediT roles.
